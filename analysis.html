A REPRODUCIBLE SIGNATURE

What the 40 coefficients mean

For each window, let N be the number of occupied bins and pᵢ the fraction of hotspot records in bin i. H = −Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ measures distribution entropy.

D₀ = ln(Nfine / Ncoarse) / ln(s)
D₁ = (Hfine − Hcoarse) / ln(s)

The fixed vector is ordered as 10 spatial D₀, 10 spatial D₁, 10 temporal D₀, 10 temporal D₁. Spatial transitions are H3 5→6 through 14→15; temporal transitions are TIX 22→23 through 31→32. The spatial length factor s is √7; the temporal factor is 2. Spatial D values use the nominal H3 scale ratio, including near pentagons.

TIX level L has duration 240−L seconds. Bins use the native dyadic epoch (Unix seconds + 239), never the start of the event. Marginal spatial bins ignore time; marginal temporal bins ignore space. The 11×11 heatmap and joint graph retain both coordinates.

The graph begins at the deepest common H3 and TIX ancestors of the full loaded event. If H3 base cells differ, its root is explicitly a union. Each layer refines the selected axes by one native level. X orders observed H3 leaves by index, preserving hierarchy groups; it is not a longitude or distance. Z is the mean detection time in a node, Y is refinement depth. Sphere volume is proportional to record count, with a minimum visible radius.

To keep navigation responsive, very large graphs display the most populated branches with their ancestors. The layer counts, heatmap and signature always use every included record. Change depth to inspect coarser or finer structure. Root and coordinate layout stay fixed for comparison. Precision limits are recomputed for the selected satellites and stay fixed as the time window moves.

These are finite-scale occupancy and entropy slopes, not fitted asymptotic fractal dimensions or a validated classifier. Satellite cadence, detection resolution, event size and repeated observations influence the signature. Levels finer than the least precise stored record are null, not zero. Empty windows are also null. Exports include the validity mask, record counts, source mix and window for comparison.

Observed-power rows group records by satellite, H3 cell and stored timestamp, sum valid non-negative pixel FRP within each group, then average over timestamps with valid detections. Invalid FRP values are excluded from power calculations but retained in hotspot counts. Each satellite remains separate: no cross-sensor energy total is computed. Pixel footprints, cloud masks and clear-sky observation coverage are required before estimating area-normalized power or time-integrated energy. H3 cell area is not a substitute for the measured sensor pixel footprint.

Playback reveals developing structure; it does not predict future growth. For precursor research, compare equally aged windows and control for satellite coverage.
